I loved these 96 days on the PCT: the landscapes, the encounters, the daily on the trail and all the challenge that this crossing represented. The difficult moments were part of it and also helped to make the experience so intense.

Above all, I end with the feeling of having taken a lot of pleasure in this experience, from the first to the last day. As soon as I get home, I already want to go back, discover another trail and live a new adventure (CDT?).
