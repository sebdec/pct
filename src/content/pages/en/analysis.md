Why is thru-hiking cool?

What I loved is the mixture of loneliness, intensity and simplicity. The days are very physical (on average not far from 30 miles - 50 km a day), in the midst of landscapes and animals that change constantly, but they are also punctuated with encounters. You can walk alone for hours, then find familiar faces at a source, campsite or resupply.

There is always something to do: move forward, seek water, eat, plan, repair, choose where to sleep, but the stress is different from that of everyday life: more concrete, more immediate. Sport, freedom, intermittent social life, planning and increasing obsession with food eventually form a very fast pace.

##

- **Solitude.** For hours alone, with enough space to let the thoughts rest, we do not see time passing.

- **Intensity.** Full days, where effort, weather and decisions matter immediately, you rarely get bored.

- **Sport.** The satisfaction of moving forward every day and seeing the body adapt to distance.

- **Landscapes.** Desert, Sierra, forests, flowers, impressive trees, climbs of pass, volcanoes, ridges and constant changes of scale.

- **Wildlife.** The animals crossed and the sensation of temporarily inhabiting their environment: bears, wolves, squirrels, deer, butterflies, birds...

- **Social life.** Short or repeated encounters, trail names and groups that form and then separate. I was surprised at the sympathy, kindness and relationship I could have with the trail angels and other hikers.

- **Planning.** The permanent balance between ambition, water, food, weather, time and physical condition.

- **Still something to do.** Walk, filter, eat, repair, organize the next day and look for a camp.

- **A different stress.** Very concrete problems, often tiring, but rarely abstract and every resolution seems like a challenge, a game.

- **Food.** resupply, cravings, rations, burgers, pizzas and learning calories - we enjoy eating a lot more.

- **Equipment.** Optimize, organize, repair its equipment to be lightweight but comfortable and efficient.
