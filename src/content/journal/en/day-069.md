First full day in Oregon, around 37 miles. I sleep the day before in a location near a lake, with at least dry toilets. The next day takes place almost entirely in the forest, without a clear view or particularly memorable scene.

I cross several section hikers and some thru-hikers: Infra, which continues until the end of Washington. Fluffy and Pogo are only about a day ahead of me, sign that I'm starting to catch up with other Nobos.
