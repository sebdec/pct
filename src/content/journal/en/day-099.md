The next day, I'm going on a bus with another hiker already crossed two days earlier. Two connections from Mazama. Instead of waiting for the next bus, I stop to Chelan, then look for another route to Wenatchee.

The chance works one last time: I fall on several people of the Lions Den that Tracy leads precisely to Wenatchee. We take four of the same buses to Seattle. Once I arrive, I order a Uber to my Fremont hotel.
