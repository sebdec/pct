Time deteriorated again after leaving François and Madeleine. Snow starts again and I'm almost all day in the footsteps of another hiker, Gavin, 23. My phone drops to 15% and then dumps abruptly, which deprives me of a map on an entirely white land. Walking with him then becomes particularly valuable.

We go up to 20:30 and clear the snow to install the tents. The night is probably the coldest since the departure, not far from -10°C: shoes, socks and bags are fully frozen at wake-up. Fortunately, the phone eventually accepts the charge and I can follow the GPS again.

The last step to Sonora Pass remains difficult: deep snow, fog, lack of traces and elevation. At times, everything is white and only the navigation on the phone allows you to keep the route. I open the passage alone to the road. This is the last major event in the Sierra.

At Sonora Pass, I find a car in a few minutes. The driver, whose trailer name is Houdini, has himself travelled through the PCT years before. He takes me to Kennedy Meadows North, where I can finally take a shower, do laundry, eat and sleep in a real bed.
