The terrain returns to an alternation of forests, climbs and descents with few views. I finish my audio book and find the day less stimulating than the day before. There are few campites in the area, as well as flies and mice.

I'm preparing the next day's stop at Snoqualmie Pass. On July 14, France played against Spain at 12:00 and I hope to find a venue to broadcast the game. It will also be necessary to buy about 6 days of food for a difficult advertised section: landslides, destroyed bridges, strong rivers and numerous blowdowns.

Once this stage has passed, the next major landmark will be Stehekin, then the Canadian border and the return to Mazama. The end of the journey begins to become close enough to be planned.
