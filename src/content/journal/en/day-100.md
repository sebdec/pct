I discover a city with great energy. The presence of Amazon, Google and many technology companies is visible, as is the aviation legacy of the region. Fremont, where I spend my first night, has an artistic, alternative and lively atmosphere. The next day, I live near the waterfront and the Downtown.

I visit Pike Place Market and its fish market, play several games at the Seattle Pinball Museum and pass by the Space Needle without going up, the ticket seems too expensive. A shooting takes place in the area a few hours after my passage, without me living it directly.

The beautiful weather and weekend animation give Seattle a very pleasant face. I also note a marked contrast between the wealth of some neighbourhoods and the visible presence of homeless people or people in great distress. Despite this, I keep a very positive impression of the city.

After these two days of transition, I take my flight to Paris.
