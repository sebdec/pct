When I leave **Cajon Pass**, I continue beyond the planned campsite, hoping to find a spot not listed in FarOut. After a long climb, I finally have to improvise a camp slightly away from the trail. The place is better than expected: a beautiful view, mild temperatures and almost no wind. I meet **Paz**, a Québécois, there.

I then reach **Wrightwood** on foot via a side trail rather than hitchhiking. I eat a huge burger with fries, several Cokes and a milkshake that is impossible to finish. I also find **Patrick** and **Patrice**, who tell me that I left my spoon at the campsite 2 days earlier. I get a plastic one and complete my resupply at a gas station.

Returning to the PCT requires almost 5 kilometres of climb, in full heat and with a much too full stomach.
