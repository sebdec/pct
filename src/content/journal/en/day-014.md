The night before, **Adam**, **Buzz** and **Atlas** choose to sleep without tent: in "cowboy camping". For my part, I settled near **Patrick** and **Patrice**, an American and the first Frenchman I met on the trail. Their rhythm is slower and we separate the next day.

I'm joining the McDonalds of Cajon Not. The place is a classic PCT hikers appointment: I'm having a feast and snuggle many times in Coke. When I leave, I discover that I have lost my water filter and its bottle. Without an immediate solution, I prepare to continue without filtering until the next city.

Fortunately, I find **Adam**, **Atlas** and **Buzz** at the exit. **Buzz** had picked up the filter at the place we stopped earlier. I leave alone, with **Buzz**'s foot problems and their slower pace, I don't know if we'll meet again.
