After a hot day, I find an almost perfect campsite with water and little wind. I meet a Belgian hiker named **Jeff**, and we hike together for the day.

The 1st miles the next morning are difficult. A storm has damaged the trail along a section called "Mission Creek", and I have to cross the river dozens of times, repeatedly looking for the best route. I find a bank card in a stream belonging to someone named **David**. My mission for the day is to return it to him. After asking and passing about 20 people, I finally catch up with him.

I push on to mile 256 to get closer to **Big Bear**. The night in the forest is perfectly calm, but freezing. The down around my feet freezes slightly and icicles form inside my bottle.
